Unknown Armies: no initiative roll — declare actions; on a tie the higher rating used goes first, and a Provides Initiative identity always counts (Init). Wounds count up to the wound threshold. After a percentile attack, pick the weapon under the Dice Roller to apply the wounds.

Basic Roleplaying: powers go first, in INT rank order; then actions in DEX rank order, highest first. Settings → BRP combat order picks which rank the tracker uses. On a tie the higher relevant skill goes first — drag the rows. A new combatant goes in at its rank; change a rank for this combat (for example after a spell), then Sort by.

Fate: the GM picks the turn order (drag the rows). Click a stress box to check it; type a consequence in the panel below. Taken out marks a combatant out of the conflict; Clear stress empties every box at the end of the conflict.

          Grid

          To set the grid from the map: choose , then drag over one cell of the map's own grid, from corner to corner. For more precision, drag over several cells and enter their number in Drag covers. For hexes, drag across a row of hexes, from the left edge of the first to the right edge of the last. Arrow keys then move the grid by 1 px (Shift: 10 px).

          Tokens
          Click an icon to put a token in the middle of the view.

          With the hand tool: drag a token to move it (Alt: no snap). Shift+click adds a token to the selection; Shift+drag selects with a box. Delete removes the selection. Right-click a token to change its label, color, or link, or to delete it. Alt+click on the map pings a point.

          Hotkeys. Alt is the Alt key on Windows and the Option (⌥) key on Mac.

          • Alt+1 to Alt+4: hand, ruler, grid, and pin tools.
          • Alt+5 to Alt+0, then Alt+-: the seven drawing tools (pen, line, rectangle, ellipse, polygon, text, eraser).
          • Alt+=: the hexcrawl tool (binoculars), when Hexcrawl is on. There are 12 tools and only 10 digits, so the last two tools use - and =.
          • Alt+S: sticky mode. Alt+F: show the whole map. Alt+O: pop out.
          • Alt+G and Alt+K: open the Grid and Tokens menus.

          Other keys.

          • Delete or Backspace: delete the selected tokens, drawing, or pin.
          • Space + drag, or a middle-button drag: move the view. With right-click pan on, a right-button drag also moves it.
          • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z (Cmd on Mac): undo and redo.
          • Escape: cancel the measure or the polygon, or close a menu.

          Settings

          Campaign and game system

          Saved in this campaign. Each campaign has its own System and rules options.

          Campaign  
          System Which game system you're running. Every tab lists only this system's characters, monsters, Compendium entries, and scenes. You can also change it in the header, next to the campaign. Everything created before this setting existed is OSR.
          WWN spell text Which text the Compendium shows for a spell that has a condensed version (from the condensed spell pack). Book — the book name and text. Condensed — the short name and text; a [Book Name] link still finds it.
          BRP combat order Which rank the Combat tracker sorts by, highest first. DEX rank (default) — the order of actions (core book p.123). INT rank — the order of powers, which go before actions (p.124); use it for a round of powers, or with the “Powers in the Action Phase” option.

          Look and sound

          The style theme is saved in this campaign. 3D dice and the roll sound are saved in this browser only.

          Style theme Recolours and re-fonts the whole app. Dark mode only. Fantasy — parchment & gold on mahogany; Sci-fi — cyan on slate, wide caps; Horror — bone & blood on near-black.
          Dice skin The look of the 3D dice. Theme color (default) uses the style theme's accent color. Random gives each die of a roll its own skin. A change throws two dice to show it. Saved in this browser only, not in the campaign.

          Setup: Dropbox app key
          1. In the Dropbox App Console, click Create app. Choose Scoped access and App folder, and give the app a name.
          2. On the Permissions tab, tick files.content.write and files.content.read. Click Submit.
          3. On the Settings tab, keep Allow public clients (Implicit Grant & PKCE) on Allow. Under Redirect URIs, add and click Add. Add the address of each place where you open the app.
          4. Copy the App key and paste it above. The app key is not a secret. Do not use the app secret.

          If you cannot add a redirect URI, : Dropbox shows a code, and you paste it here.

                Add monster

                  Paste one or more stat blocks (Shadowdark / OSE). Leave a blank line between monsters.

                  Edit monster —

                  Saving re-parses (Paste text) or rebuilds (Fill in fields) the stat block, keeping this monster’s place in the library. Combatants already added to the tracker are not changed.

                  Fate NPC generator

                  Fate has no challenge rating: an NPC's difficulty is its peak skill compared with the PCs' peak skill. The same seed always makes the same NPC.

                  Populate a Fate encounter

                  Easy: one threat and a mob of nameless NPCs. Standard: two or three threats, a hitter, and a mob. Climactic: a boss, four or five threats, a hitter, and a mob. Fewer threats for 1–2 PCs.

                  Populate by HD

                  Spend a total Hit Dice budget on monsters picked from the current Bestiary. A monster with 0 HD still costs 0.5 HD of the budget. The whole budget is always spent exactly.

                  Spend a total Encounter Level (EL) budget on monsters picked from the current Bestiary. EL 1 is a fair fight for one starting Normal-level PC, and ELs add up. Monsters without an EL are not picked. The whole budget is always spent exactly.

                  Review the proposed monsters, then confirm to add them to combat.
